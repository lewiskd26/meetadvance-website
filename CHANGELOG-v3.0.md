# MeetAdvance Website v3.0 Changelog

## Release focus
Competitive-positioning and go-to-market pivot following validation of an incumbent general sports-management competitor.

## Positioning
- Defines MeetAdvance as **the post-results operations platform for track & field**.
- Adds the message: **Track doesn't end when the results are posted. Neither should your operations platform.**
- Emphasizes coexistence with registration, timing, scoring, results, and broader event-management systems instead of rip-and-replace.
- Adds a clear technology-stack distinction: Registration/Event Setup → Timing/Scoring/Results → MeetAdvance.
- Reframes the target audience around governing organizations, championship hosts, meet directors, and track clubs.

## Company identity
- Adds: **Black-owned. Track-focused. Built around the people who operate the sport.**
- Keeps ownership as a company/supplier differentiator while track specialization and operational depth remain the product case.

## Product communication
- Adds a “What happens after results?” section centered on qualifiers, fees, reconciliation, protests, awards, and meet closure.
- Compresses public AthleteVerify™ detail into Eligibility, Verification, and Integrity capability categories.
- Preserves current-vs-planned labeling.
- Strengthens fictional-demo disclaimers for screenshots that contain historical staging labels.

## Organizations and GTM
- Removes TAAF-specific deployment examples as the primary organization framing.
- Replaces them with regional championship pathways, qualifier/Junior Olympic pathways, independent championship meets, and club-hosted meets.
- Notes applicability to AAU districts, USATF associations, TAAF regions, independent operators, and other track organizations without implying endorsement.

## Pilot conversion
- Replaces generic “Discuss a Pilot” language with **Bring Us Your Workflow**.
- Changes the core discovery question to: **What is still manual after your results become official?**
- Adds protest tracking and eligibility verification to pilot-interest choices.

## Commercial model
- Preserves v2.5 pricing.
- Reframes pricing around adding MeetAdvance to the existing technology stack rather than replacing incumbent systems.
