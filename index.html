Built for every meet format

Circuit-meet speed. Championship-meet control.

Different meet formats create different operational pressure. MeetAdvance supports both without forcing one workflow onto every event.

Circuit Meets

Built for high-volume ribbon distribution.

A meet with 20 events and 3–4 heats can create 60–80+ separate ribbon distributions before medals are considered.

  • Track ribbons per heat—not just per event
  • Manage continuous awards-station throughput
  • Forecast inventory from expected heat volume
  • Reduce sorting and distribution workload
Championship Meets

Accountability from qualifier to medal pickup.

Manage advancement decisions, payment obligations, medal distribution, and post-meet reconciliation.

  • Verify official placement and eligibility
  • Track individual and relay awards
  • Prevent duplicate pickup
  • Maintain a complete audit history
