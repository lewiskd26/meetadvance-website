Recommended scope

What a first pilot can include.

  • One regional championship and one defined workflow owner
  • Official results as the post-results starting point
  • Advancement decisions, deadlines, and entry-fee status
  • Individual and relay reconciliation
  • Itemized team invoice and shareable PDF
  • Optional awards readiness / secure pickup workflow
  • Pre-event validation and post-event review

Suggested success measures

  • Pending advancement decisions are visible and actionable.
  • Accepted entries reconcile to the correct team invoice total.
  • Outstanding balances are identifiable by athlete/event or relay.
  • Meet staff can receive a clean itemized PDF record.
  • Operational exceptions are visible without separate manual lists.

What MeetAdvance needs from the pilot organization

A small set of decisions before meet day.

Workflow Owner

A regional or meet-level administrator who can confirm rules and decisions.

Meet Rules

Advancement rules, deadlines, fee structure, and participating team scope.

Results Handoff

A clear process for when official results become available for post-results work.

Post-Event Review

A short review to capture issues, completion status, and whether the workflow should continue.
